SCORING SYSTEM

HOW STANDINGS WORK

We believe in total transparency — here's exactly how your position is decided.

01 🏆 WINS COME FIRST

Teams are ranked by number of match wins. In the league, win = first to 2 sets (best of 3). In Semis & Final, win = first to 3 sets (best of 5).

A walkover (opponent absent) counts as a win with score 2–0 for the present team. The team that didn't show up gets 0 sets, 0 points, and a loss recorded.

02 📊 SET RATIO — THE TIEBREAKER

If two teams have the same number of wins, Set Ratio decides their order.

Sets Won
Sets Won + Sets Lost

Higher ratio = higher position.
Example: 4 SW, 1 SL → 4÷5 = 0.80

03 🔢 POINT RATIO — LAST RESORT

If Set Ratio is also equal, total points scored across all sets decide the final ranking.

Points Scored
Points Scored + Points Conceded

This is rarely needed but ensures no two teams ever finish level.

HOW TEAMS QUALIFY FOR THE KNOCKOUT STAGE
Top 2 teams from Pool A and top 2 teams from Pool B advance to the Semifinals on Day 3.

Semifinal 1: Pool A 1st place vs Pool B 2nd place
Semifinal 2: Pool B 1st place vs Pool A 2nd place
